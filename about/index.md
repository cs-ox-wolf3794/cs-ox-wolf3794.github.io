# Shakya Deb Ganguly
**Principal Tech Project Manager | AI, Data & Advanced Analytics**

I am a Principal Tech Project Manager based in Oslo, working at the intersection of technology, data, and domain expertise to build scalable, insight-driven systems. My work focuses on leveraging advanced analytics and artificial intelligence to solve complex, real-world problems across industries such as energy and finance.

Currently, I lead technical project management and DevOps initiatives at Rystad Energy, where I am responsible for driving secure, scalable product development across cross-functional teams. My role centers on shaping data-intensive platforms and AI-enabled solutions, with a strong emphasis on data security, interoperability, and hybrid architectures. I work closely with senior leadership to define product strategy, embed AI into core workflows, and ensure systems meet high standards of robustness and compliance.

Prior to this, I was a Senior Project Manager at Cognite, where I worked on enabling SaaS adoption in asset-heavy industries by bridging operational technology (OT) and IT systems. My focus was on accelerating time-to-value through efficient data onboarding, scalable architectures, and risk-aware implementation strategies—helping organizations unlock the potential of industrial data.

Earlier in my career, I held leadership roles spanning operations, analytics, and technology. As a Senior Operations Manager, I led cross-functional teams and applied data-driven methods—such as A/B testing and predictive analytics—to improve performance and decision-making. As Head of Technology in the education sector, I worked on machine learning applications, large-scale data systems, and cybersecurity initiatives, strengthening both product capabilities and system resilience.

I began my career as a Technology Analyst at J.P. Morgan, where I developed a strong foundation in financial systems and quantitative thinking. I worked on modeling financial products, building trade-processing systems, and delivering regulatory reporting solutions across global markets. This experience shaped my ability to operate in highly regulated, data-intensive environments where precision, security, and scale are critical.

Across my career, a consistent theme has been working in complex, data-rich domains where technology alone is not enough—success requires a deep understanding of industry context. I specialize in bridging this gap, combining technical depth with domain insight to build systems that are both intelligent and practical.

In parallel, I am deeply interested in quantitative analysis and financial modeling, applying statistical and data-driven approaches to understand markets, risk, and decision-making under uncertainty. This analytical mindset underpins much of my work and informs how I approach both technology and strategy.

Beyond delivery and strategy, I am passionate about building strong teams. I mentor engineers, analysts, and product professionals, with a focus on creating a culture of clarity, ownership, and continuous improvement.

At my core, I am driven by curiosity and a desire to solve meaningful problems—bringing together technology, data, and domain expertise to create systems that deliver lasting impact.
